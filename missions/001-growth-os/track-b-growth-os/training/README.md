# Agent training — MISSION-001 Track B

These files train four NFAI agents to run **business processes** with measurable outcomes, using ready services where they help and NFAI-owned methods where they protect quality, cost, or independence (t458u). They are not “how to click a vendor UI.”

Catalog of named services: [../services-registry.md](../services-registry.md) and [../../track-a-rush-conf/services.md](../../track-a-rush-conf/services.md).  
Module strategy: [../buy-integrate-build.md](../buy-integrate-build.md).

| File | Agent | Business outcome they own |
|---|---|---|
| [prompt-engineer.md](prompt-engineer.md) | Промпт-инженер | A repeatable, believable visibility and content-question process that a director can read. |
| [market-researcher.md](market-researcher.md) | Исследователь рынка | A sourced market and competitor brief that feeds modules 01–03. |
| [integration-developer.md](integration-developer.md) | Разработчик интеграций | Working intake, tags, robots/analytics glue, and logs — without becoming a CRM vendor. |
| [orchestrator.md](orchestrator.md) | Оркестратор | The six-step work order, independent review, and stop rules (spend, production, claims). |

Acceptance tests are written so a reviewer can pass/fail them with evidence. No signups or live-site edits in training itself.
