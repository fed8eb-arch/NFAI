Document version: v1
Last updated: 2026-11-18
Status: internal working draft

## Product and intended use

NFAI is being developed as an AI-agent digital agency. The current stage is internal testing. The presentation checking workflow accepts a PPTX and a required slide count, then checks the slide count and whether at least one slide contains native text. Visual quality and other elements require separate review.

Evidence: [presentation acceptance](../projects/03-telegram-tooling/results/24-presentation-acceptance.md), [internal Reels pilot](../projects/03-telegram-tooling/results/nov15-reels-editorial-pilot.md).

## Audience and objective for the internal pilot

For the presentation-checking demonstration, the pilot addresses a hypothetical customer who needs a slide deck checked against a brief. Its objective is to explain the checks and their limits. This is a working exercise, and audience demand, pricing, conversion rate and paid customers are unverified.

## Claims available to content creators

- An internal two-slide test passed a two-slide check; a one-slide test did not. The evidence is in the internal Reels pilot.
- The current checker looks for native text on at least one slide. It does not establish that all elements are editable.
- Layout, colors, content and typography need visual review.

Do not infer client outcomes, revenue, speed, market demand or platform reach without measured evidence. Verify any claim against the current implementation before using it.

## Workflow for marketing agents

When drafting content for the internal presentation-checking pilot, read this file and the linked evidence first. State the audience, purpose and desired action in the brief. Mark unknown customer facts as unknown. Draft original content and link factual claims to evidence. Submit for review under the agency's existing publication permissions.
