# YouTube video research

1. Identify the public video URL and ID. Check the project review and results for the same ID and overlapping techniques. Record the requested period separately from the video's publication date.
2. Use an available video connector for a first pass. If the connector cannot retrieve the source or its quota is exhausted, open the video in an authorized browser, using the supported browser controls.
3. On YouTube, inspect the current video for the ✦ **Ask / Задавайте вопросы** button under the video. The button was verified on the watch page for Zzt_lgDb5-U: it opened a panel whose **Краткий пересказ видео** option returned a timestamped summary. The Shorts view did not show this button on two checked videos; check the same video's standard watch page when necessary. When present, open it and request a short summary or ask a specific question about the exact tool, settings and prerequisites. The button and AI answer must actually appear before claiming they were used. Availability can differ by video and interface.
4. Expand the description using **more / ещё**, then open **Show transcript / Показать текст видео** when available. Read the timestamped segments relevant to the task. Playback captions and the transcript panel are separate controls; check the panel even if captions are unavailable.
5. Cross-check AI summaries against the transcript, video frames or audio, and the original tool documentation. Transcripts can misrecognize names, commands and numbers. Distinguish the author's claim, the AI-generated answer and independently verified facts. Record the exact source and timestamp, plus any inaccessible material.
6. Compare each relevant technique with existing NFAI capabilities. For new, applicable work, identify the owner and prerequisites, check license, cost and access, implement and test through the normal workflow. Record results and limitations. Give the responsible agent a practical task after implementation.
7. Save non-sensitive findings, evidence and the status of each source in the project. Commit verified changes to NFAI and confirm the published file. Do not put private chat contents, credentials or account information in the public repository.
